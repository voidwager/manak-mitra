1.0 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The hydrocracker unit HCU-I is operating at 98.4 percent of its design
capacity utilisation, against a 2026 target of 95.0 percent. Nameplate
capacity for the demo refinery complex is 15 MMTPA.

The unit has exceeded its availability target for six consecutive quarters.
The principal constraint on further throughput is the heat exchanger train,
specifically E-302, whose fouling rate governs the interval between
turnarounds.

2.0 COMMERCIAL POSITION

2.1 Margin

Unit gross margin for the trailing twelve months was 4,820 INR per tonne
against a plan of 4,150 INR per tonne. The variance is attributed primarily
to feedstock selection rather than to operational improvement, and is not
assumed to persist into the 2027 plan.

2.2 Turnaround economics

The next major turnaround is scheduled for the third quarter of 2027 at an
estimated cost of 1,840 million INR and 28 days of lost production. Deferral
by one quarter has been evaluated and is not recommended: the E-302 fouling
trend indicates the exchanger would become the binding constraint before the
deferred date, forfeiting more margin than the deferral saves.

2.3 Capital proposal

A proposal for a parallel exchanger train at an estimated 3,200 million INR
is under evaluation. Preliminary analysis indicates a payback of 4.1 years
at current margins, which does not meet the 3.0 year hurdle. The proposal is
not recommended for the 2027 capital programme in its present form.

3.0 RISK REGISTER EXTRACT

3.1 Single point of failure

The unit has no installed spare for the recycle gas compressor. A failure
requiring a rotor replacement would take the unit down for an estimated 21
days. The mitigation - a shared spare rotor held at a regional store - has
been agreed in principle but is not yet contracted.

3.2 Catalyst

The current catalyst load is at 71 percent of its expected life. Performance
is tracking slightly ahead of the deactivation curve. No change to the
replacement schedule is recommended at this review.

3.3 Personnel

Two of the four senior rotating equipment specialists are within three years
of retirement. Succession cover is assessed as inadequate and is the highest
rated non-technical risk on the unit register.

4.0 RECOMMENDATIONS

Proceed with the 2027 third quarter turnaround as scheduled.
Do not proceed with the parallel exchanger train as currently specified.
Contract the shared spare rotor arrangement before the end of 2026.
Commission a succession plan for the rotating equipment section.
