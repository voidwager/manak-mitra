## 1. When the assistant's answer is not enough

A visitor question is escalated to a certification officer when any of the
following apply (escalation code CD-7):

1. The visitor asks whether their specific product needs a licence and the
   product is not on a published compulsory-certification list.
2. The visitor reports that a licence application has been pending for more
   than 30 days.
3. The visitor disputes a fee.

## 2. Target response times (sample)

- CD-7 escalations: first reply within 2 working days.
- Pending-application queries: status within 5 working days.

## 3. What officers must not do

Officers do not tell a visitor that a licence "will be granted". Grant is
decided by the licensing branch after inspection and testing.
